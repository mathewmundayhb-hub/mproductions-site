Terms & Cancellation

These terms explain the 50/50 payment structure, revisions, cancellation, website accounts and how the final balance works. Last updated: 5 September 2026.

50% deposit, then you decide

You pay 50% of the full project price when you book. After you receive the first revision, you decide whether you want to continue working with Mproductions. If you continue, Mproductions sends a secure Stripe payment link by email for the remaining 50%.

The second 50% is not taken automatically from your card or bank account. You must actively open and pay the second Stripe link.

1. Services

These terms apply to mixing, mastering, production, editing, vocal tuning, drum alignment, synth work and related audio-production services purchased from Mproductions.

2. Price and the 50/50 payment plan

  • The website shows the full project price before checkout, including any multi-track discount.
  • 50% of that full project price is payable when the booking is made.
  • The remaining 50% is only due after the first revision if the client chooses to continue.
  • If the client chooses to continue, Mproductions emails the balance-payment link to the email address supplied at Stripe checkout.
  • The client makes the second payment themselves through Stripe. Mproductions does not automatically debit the remaining balance.
  • The second payment completes payment for the originally agreed project, unless the client later requests additional work outside the original scope.

3. Email and payment information

Stripe Checkout asks for the client's email address before payment details. Mproductions uses the booking email for payment administration, project communication and, where appropriate, sending the remaining 50% payment link after the first revision. Mproductions does not receive or store the client's full card number.

4. First revision and continuation

The first revision is the decision point for the second payment. After receiving it, the client can either continue or stop.

  • Continue: the client confirms they wish to continue and pays the remaining 50% using the Stripe link sent by Mproductions.
  • Stop: the client does not make the second payment. Any refund due from the initial 50% is determined by these terms and applicable consumer law, including the work already carried out.

5. Consumer cancellation rights

If the client is contracting as a consumer online, statutory cancellation rights may apply, including a 14-day cancellation period in relevant circumstances. If the client expressly asks Mproductions to begin work during that cancellation period and later cancels after work has begun, the client may have to pay a proportionate amount for services already supplied, as permitted by law.

These terms do not remove or reduce any statutory rights that cannot legally be excluded.

6. Deposit and refunds

The initial 50% is part-payment toward the agreed project and is not described as automatically non-refundable in every situation. Where a cancellation or refund request is made, the amount due back will depend on the circumstances, applicable law and the amount of work already performed.

7. Revisions and changes of scope

The selected package includes the stated number of revisions. A revision means a reasonable set of changes within the agreed project direction. Replacement recordings, a substantially changed brief, new production requirements or significant additional work can require a separate quote.

8. Turnaround and client materials

Typical delivery is usually within two weeks, but timing can vary according to project size, response times, technical issues and revision requests. The client confirms that they have the right to provide the recordings, stems, samples and other materials supplied to Mproductions.

9. Final delivery

Where the client chooses to continue after the first revision, final approved masters and final production deliverables are released after the project is complete and all amounts due for the agreed project have been paid.

10. How to cancel

Send a clear cancellation request to mathewmundayhb@gmail.com and include the booking name, booking email, artist/project name and booking date.

12. Website accounts

Mproductions may offer optional website accounts. Account authentication is provided through Supabase. When a client creates an account, the account service stores information such as the name and email address supplied for login and account identification.

The website may keep a signed-in session on the client’s device so the client does not need to sign in on every visit. Clients can sign out at any time from the account page. Account details are separate from Stripe card data; Mproductions does not receive or store a client’s full card number through the website account system.
