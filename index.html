What Happens After Booking

50% Now. You Control The Rest.

The second half is not taken automatically. You only make the final 50% payment after you have heard your first revision and decided you want to continue.

01

Choose & Book

Select your package and number of tracks. The website shows the full project total and your 50% booking deposit.

02

Email + Stripe

Stripe asks for your email before your payment method, along with your artist and project details. You then pay the 50% deposit securely.

03

Send Your Stems

Send stems, notes and reference tracks. Your booking confirmation explains the next steps clearly.

04

First Revision

You receive your first revision while only the deposit has been paid.

05

You Decide

If you want to continue, Mproductions emails the remaining 50% Stripe payment link to the email used at checkout. If you do not continue, no second payment is taken.

06

Finish The Project

You actively open the second Stripe link and make the final 50% payment. The remaining included revisions and final delivery then continue.

No automatic second charge Mproductions does not automatically take the remaining 50% from your bank or card. The client must choose to continue and actively pay the second secure Stripe link.
